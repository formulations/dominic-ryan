A Personal Message

I’m 𝐃𝐨𝐦𝐢𝐧𝐢𝐜 𝐑𝐲𝐚𝐧.

Based in Austin, Texas. I’ve always been drawn to things that carry a point of view — objects, places, images, and ideas shaped by intention.

My professional life has been shaped by nearly a decade of experience in refined interiors, creative strategy, and advertising. Today, I lead teams and develop strategy in paid social, where I practice at the intersection of media, culture, and human behavior.

Campaigns under my strategic leadership have received global recognition from the renowned Cannes Lions®, The New York Times®, the Effie Awards®, and the Festival of Media — experiences that have deepened my understanding of what makes an idea resonate and how thoughtful decisions can shape the way people perceive, feel, and engage.

That instinct follows me outside of work. I have always noticed design: proportion, material, patina, typography, architecture, the composition of a room, or the evidence of a hand behind an object. I collect decorative objects, archival books, timepieces, crystal, alabaster, porcelain, brass, and pieces from esteemed maisons — not according to convention, but according to instinct.

𝑭𝒐𝒓𝒎𝒖𝒍𝒂𝒕𝒊𝒐𝒏𝒔 is a private and highly considered collection of that way of seeing — formulations of art, design, travel, photography, and objects with a past. It is also, in some sense, a record of becoming: what I notice, what I value, what I learn, and what I hope to create next.

Much like myself, the moments I visit are treated with considerable discretion. Some things are photographed. Some are remembered. Some are shared only within my closest circle.
