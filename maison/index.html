THE AUBREMONT RESIDENCE

THE AUBREMONT RESIDENCEAn interior shaped through considered choices in proportion, material, furnishings, and atmosphere.

A REALIZED INTERIOR

An interior,
thoughtfully
composed.

There is a certain privilege in being entrusted with the character of a space. Through my work on the Aubremont Residence, I sought an interior that felt settled rather than staged — a place where furnishings, architectural details, and objects could sit comfortably alongside one another.

I paid particular attention to proportion: the placement of each piece, the way materials meet, and how light changes the character of the room throughout the day. Texture, tone, and a few carefully chosen details give the space its own personality without asking too much of it.

What interests me most about interiors is often what takes time to notice. The film captures some of those quieter decisions — and the pleasure of seeing a room come together.

— DOMINIC RYAN
THE SIGNATURE / 02

Two letters.
One point of view.

There is something deeply personal about creating a space. The Aubremont Residence reflects choices I made, instincts I followed, and an understanding of beauty that has become my own over time. Yet those qualities aren’t confined to interiors. They follow me into the things I collect, the places I visit, and the ideas I choose to explore.

The DR monogram is perhaps the simplest expression of that connection. Not a representation of any one interest or discipline, but of the person to whom they all belong. Two letters, drawn from something more enduring than any particular creation: my name.
