THE RESIDENCE — An interior design study in classical proportion, architectural symmetry, and the quiet interplay of light, texture, and material. A personal exploration of how considered details shape the atmosphere of a home.

THE RESIDENCE   /   AN INTERIOR DESIGN STUDY

Considered in every detail.

For me, an interior is more than a collection of beautiful objects. It is an expression of proportion, material, memory, and the way a space is experienced.

This film is a study of those relationships: classical architecture softened by light; the permanence of stone and wood balanced by the intimacy of furnishings and collected objects. Each composition reflects an instinct for restraint, harmony, and the evidence of intention.

Rather than presenting a finished architectural commission, The Residence offers a visual exploration of the interior design sensibility behind The Maison.
